How to update the forecast S-curve
  1. Click Download Forecast Template (CSV). The file opens in Excel and is already filled with the forecast now on the chart.
  2. Edit column Forecast Cumulative (RM) — key in your own programme figures. Column Period Label is optional (e.g. Jan-2026) and is only used for the chart labels.
  3. Add or delete rows freely. The chart re-scales to however many periods you leave in the file.
  4. Save as CSV, then click Upload Updated Forecast and pick the file. The orange line, the red forecast marker and the ahead/behind note all refresh straight away.
  5. Your uploaded forecast is remembered on this PC. Click Reset to Default to go back to the original curve.

Rules: figures must be cumulative (running total, always increasing). Commas and "RM" are fine — they are stripped automatically. Blank rows are skipped.
